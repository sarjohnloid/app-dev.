  ## TransFormers Overview 
A transformer is a passive electromagnetic device that transfers AC electrical energy between circuits using electromagnetic induction, changing voltage and current levels according to its coil turns ratio. It was invented by Michael Faraday in 1831 and is essential in power transmission, distribution, and electronic systems.
